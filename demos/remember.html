Robert “Bob” Hale

1948 – 2026 · Husband, father, and the world’s worst fisherman

Share a memory

Bob believed a bad day fishing beat a good day anywhere else, and he lived like it. He is survived by his wife of 51 years, Carol, their three kids, and seven grandkids who all know exactly how he liked his coffee.

He never met a stranger at the lake, never let a grandkid lose at checkers on purpose, and never — not once — caught the big one he spent forty years talking about.

How it works

About five minutes of your time.

  1. Tell Nova what’s happening

    Nova is our chat assistant at trymayfly.com/start. It asks for the date, the place, a few photos, and how long the page should stay up.

  2. We build it

    A person reviews every order before it’s built from one of the eight designs. The link arrives by email, usually within a few hours.

  3. Share it, then let it go

    Text the link, post it, or print the QR code on a flyer. On the last day the page comes down and its photos are deleted.

Questions

Things people ask.

What happens when time runs out?

The page and its photos are deleted. Anyone who opens the link after that sees a short note saying the page has expired.

Can I add more time?

Yes. Email mayfly@agentmail.to before the page expires and we’ll extend it at the same prices.

Who builds the page?

Nova collects the details in the chat. Every order is then reviewed before the page is built and published, which is why it takes a few hours.

What won’t you build?

Anything lewd, hateful, or dangerous, and anything about drugs, alcohol, gambling, or weapons. If we can’t build your page, we’ll say so.

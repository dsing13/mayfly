Things people ask.

What happens when time runs out?

The page and its photos are deleted. Anyone who opens the link after that sees a short note saying the page has expired.

Can I add more time?

Yes. Email mayfly@agentmail.to before the page expires and we’ll extend it at the same prices.

Who builds the page?

You answer a few questions in the form. Every order is then reviewed before the page is built and published, which is why it takes about 2 hours (about 10 minutes with Rush).

What won’t you build?

Anything lewd, hateful, or dangerous, and anything about drugs, alcohol, gambling, or weapons. If we can’t build your page, we’ll say so.

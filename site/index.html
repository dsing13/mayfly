Questions

Fair things to ask.

What happens when it expires?

It evaporates. The page comes down, the link stops working, and nothing lingers — no archives, no "renew now" emails chasing you. Like it was never there, because it didn't need to be.

Can I extend it?

Yes. Email us before the clock runs out and we'll add time at the same simple rates. After it's gone, it's gone — so don't sleep on it.

What content do you refuse?

Anything lewd, vulgar, racist, hateful, dangerous, or gross — and nothing about vice. Mayfly sites are one-shot pages for fun and genuinely useful information. If it's mean or sketchy, it's a no, and we'll tell you so kindly.
